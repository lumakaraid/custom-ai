# VISUAL PROMPT GUIDE
# How to Write Super Detailed Visual Prompts for AI Image Generation

Visual prompts are for slides 1, 2, and 5 ONLY. These prompts will be used with AI image generators like Gemini, Dreamina, or Whisk to create high-quality, on-brand images.

---

## VISUAL PROMPT STRUCTURE

A super detailed visual prompt must include ALL of these elements:

### 1. Core Scene (WHO + WHAT + WHERE)
- **Subject:** Indonesian entrepreneur/professional (age, gender, attire)
- **Action:** What they're doing (specific, not generic)
- **Setting:** Where this is happening (specific location details)

### 2. Visual Metaphor
- What does this image represent conceptually?
- What feeling or idea does it convey?
- How does it relate to the slide's message?

### 3. Composition
- **Rule of thirds:** Where is the subject positioned?
- **Leading lines:** What guides the eye?
- **Depth:** Foreground, midground, background elements
- **Framing:** How is the scene framed?

### 4. Background & Environment
- **Setting details:** Indonesian workspace, modern office, co-working space
- **Cultural touches:** Subtle Indonesian elements (batik pattern, traditional art, local plants)
- **Professional atmosphere:** Clean, modern, organized
- **Props:** Laptop, notebook, coffee, plants, books

### 5. Lighting
- **Type:** Natural daylight, soft diffused, warm rim light
- **Color temperature:** 5000K-5500K (daylight), 3000K-4000K (warm)
- **Direction:** From window, overhead, side lighting
- **Mood:** Bright and optimistic, calm and focused, warm and inviting
- **Shadows:** Gentle shadows for depth, not harsh

### 6. Color Palette (Lumakara Brand Colors)
Must specify how EACH brand color is used in the scene:

- **Sage Green (#8FA98F):** Growth, fresh perspective
  - Usage: Plant elements, accent wall, cushions, subtle background
  
- **Muted Teal (#6FA5A0):** Professional clarity, cool intellect
  - Usage: Laptop screen glow, book covers, wall art, tech elements
  
- **Burnt Sand (#D7A98C):** Warmth, human connection
  - Usage: Warm wood desk tones, coffee cup, leather notebook, skin tones
  
- **Indigo Grey (#8690A6):** Professional atmosphere, tech-forward
  - Usage: Shadows, background walls, clothing accents, tech devices

### 7. Mood & Atmosphere
- **Emotional tone:** Calm confidence, strategic thinking, fresh start energy
- **Feeling:** Focused, inspired, determined, hopeful, professional
- **Energy level:** Calm and composed, energized but not frantic
- **Vibe:** Modern professional, approachable expert, relatable entrepreneur

### 8. Technical Specifications
- **Lens:** 35mm (environmental context) or 50mm (portrait focus)
- **Aperture:** f/2.8 (shallow depth of field) or f/5.6 (more in focus)
- **Photography style:** Hyper-realistic lifestyle photography, authentic Indonesian business setting
- **Focus:** Sharp focus on subject, gentle bokeh background
- **Quality:** High resolution, professional photography, editorial quality

## SLIDE-SPECIFIC GUIDELINES

### Slide 1: Hook/Opening
**Purpose:** Grab attention, inspire, show fresh start energy

**Mood:** Inspiring, professional, strategic thinking, fresh perspective

**Example Subjects:**
- Entrepreneur reviewing strategy on laptop with confident expression
- Professional writing in notebook with thoughtful focus
- Business owner looking at vision board with determination
- Founder in modern workspace with calm confidence

**Key Elements:**
- Subject on left third (rule of thirds)
- Negative space on right suggesting growth/possibility
- Morning light (fresh start energy)
- Clean, organized workspace
- Subtle Indonesian cultural elements

### Slide 2: Problem/Challenge
**Purpose:** Show relatable struggle, authentic business challenge

**Mood:** Realistic, relatable, thoughtful, problem-solving mode

**Example Subjects:**
- Entrepreneur analyzing data with concerned but focused expression
- Professional surrounded by notes, working through challenge
- Business owner in deep thought, hand on chin
- Founder reviewing multiple options, decision-making moment

**Key Elements:**
- More cluttered workspace (showing complexity)
- Warmer lighting (human struggle)
- Subject centered or slightly off-center
- Props showing work in progress (notes, charts, multiple screens)
- Relatable, not dramatic

### Slide 5: Pitfall/Warning
**Purpose:** Show wisdom, protective guidance, cautionary but constructive

**Mood:** Cautionary, wise, protective, experienced guidance

**Example Subjects:**
- Mentor figure pointing out important detail
- Professional reviewing mistake with learning expression
- Experienced entrepreneur sharing wisdom
- Business owner with "lesson learned" expression

**Key Elements:**
- Subject positioned as guide/advisor
- Warmer, more intimate lighting
- Props showing experience (books, awards, established workspace)
- Subtle warning signs (red flag, caution symbol) very subtle
- Wise, not scary

Color Palette: Sage Green (#8FA98F) in succulent plant and accent wall visible in background, representing growth and fresh perspective. Muted Teal (#6FA5A0) in laptop screen glow and book spines on shelf, suggesting professional clarity. Technical: 35mm lens for environmental context, f/2.8 aperture for shallow depth of field, sharp focus on subject with gentle bokeh background. Hyper-realistic lifestyle photography style, authentic Indonesian business setting, editorial quality. High resolution, professional photography, natural and unposed."

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

❌ **Too Generic**
"Indonesian entrepreneur working on laptop"
- Not detailed enough for AI to generate quality image

❌ **Missing Color Palette**
Forgetting to specify how Lumakara brand colors are used
- Results in off-brand images

❌ **Wrong Mood**
"Stressed entrepreneur with head in hands"
- Too negative, not aligned with Lumakara's calm, strategic vibe

❌ **No Technical Specs**
Missing lens, aperture, photography style
- Results in inconsistent quality

❌ **Too Corporate**
"Business person in suit in glass office building"
- Too formal, not relatable, not Lumakara vibe

❌ **Missing Indonesian Context**
Generic office with no cultural elements
- Doesn't resonate with target audience

## QUALITY CHECKLIST

Before finalizing a visual prompt, check:

✅ All 8 elements included (Scene, Metaphor, Composition, Background, Lighting, Colors, Mood, Technical)
✅ Specific Indonesian context and cultural elements
✅ All 4 Lumakara brand colors specified with usage
✅ Lighting type and color temperature specified
✅ Technical specs (lens, aperture, style) included
✅ Mood aligns with Lumakara brand (calm, strategic, professional, relatable)
✅ Subject is Indonesian entrepreneur/professional
✅ Setting is modern Indonesian business environment
✅ Prompt is 300+ words (detailed enough for AI)
✅ Natural and authentic, not staged or stock-photo-like

# LUMAKARA BRAND VOICE

**Personality**: Projector - "The Seer" (advisor, not vendor)
**Tone**: Calm, strategic, practical, relatable
**Language**: Conversational Indonesian mixed with English (natural for Gen Z/Millennials)
**Style**: Professional yet approachable, NOT corporate or stiff
**Pronouns**: Use "kamu" (informal), NOT "Anda" (formal)

**Voice Guidelines**:
- Speak like a thinking partner, not a lecturer
- Use real-world examples and relatable scenarios
- Balance insight with actionability
- Avoid jargon unless explaining it
- Be honest about challenges while offering hope
- Quality over quantity mindset

# CONTENT STRUCTURE

Each carousel has **6 slides**:

**Slide 1: Hook + Main Topic**
- Headline: 60-80 characters
- Body: 200-250 characters
- Visual Prompt: SUPER DETAILED (300+ words) for Gemini/Whisk
- Visual Keywords: 5-8 comma-separated terms

**Slide 2: Problem/Context**
- Headline: 60-80 characters
- Body: 200-250 characters
- Visual Prompt: SUPER DETAILED (300+ words)
- Visual Keywords: 5-8 comma-separated terms

**Slide 3: Insight/Understanding**
- Headline: 60-80 characters
- Body: 200-250 characters
- Visual Prompt: null (no detailed prompt)
- Visual Keywords: 5-8 comma-separated terms

**Slide 4: Solution/Framework**
- Headline: 60-80 characters
- Body: 200-250 characters
- Visual Prompt: null
- Visual Keywords: 5-8 comma-separated terms

**Slide 5: Pitfalls/Mistakes**
- Headline: 60-80 characters
- Body: 200-250 characters
- Visual Prompt: SUPER DETAILED (300+ words)
- Visual Keywords: 5-8 comma-separated terms

**Slide 6: Action + CTA**
- Headline: 60-80 characters
- Body: 200-250 characters
- Visual Prompt: null
- Visual Keywords: 5-8 comma-separated terms

# VISUAL PROMPT REQUIREMENTS

For slides 1, 2, and 5, create SUPER DETAILED visual prompts including:

1. **Core Scene**: Subject (Indonesian entrepreneur/professional), specific action, setting details
2. **Visual Metaphor**: What the image represents conceptually
3. **Composition**: Rule of thirds, leading lines, depth, framing
4. **Background**: Indonesian workspace, modern professional setting, cultural touches
5. **Lighting**: Natural daylight, color temperature (e.g., 5000K), mood, direction
6. **Color Palette**: 
   - Sage Green (#8FA98F) - Growth, fresh perspective
   - Muted Teal (#6FA5A0) - Professional clarity, cool intellect
   - Burnt Sand (#D7A98C) - Warmth, human connection
   - Indigo Grey (#8690A6) - Professional atmosphere, tech-forward
7. **Mood & Atmosphere**: Emotional tone, feeling, energy
8. **Technical Specs**: Lens (35mm/50mm), aperture (f/2.8), photography style

**Example Visual Prompt**:
"Scene: Indonesian entrepreneur (late 20s, professional attire) sitting at modern minimalist desk, reviewing business strategy on laptop with confident, focused expression. Setting: Contemporary co-working space with floor-to-ceiling windows, natural morning light (5500K). Composition: Rule of thirds, subject on left third, negative space on right suggesting growth. Background: Clean Indonesian workspace with subtle batik pattern cushion, modern tech balanced with natural materials. Lighting: Soft diffused daylight from large windows, gentle shadows adding depth, warm rim light on subject. Color Palette: Sage Green (#8FA98F) in plant elements and accent wall, Muted Teal (#6FA5A0) in laptop screen glow, Burnt Sand (#D7A98C) in warm wood desk tones, Indigo Grey (#8690A6) in shadows. Mood: Calm confidence, strategic thinking, fresh start energy. Technical: 35mm lens, f/2.8 aperture, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on subject with gentle bokeh background, hyper-realistic lifestyle photography, authentic Indonesian business setting."

# LUMAKARA SERVICES

## Service Philosophy

**"From Idea to Impact"**

Every business has a unique journey. Our services are designed to meet you where you are and guide you to where you want to be.

**Service Design Principles:**
1. **Stage-Appropriate:** Matches your business stage
2. **Outcome-Focused:** Clear results and deliverables
3. **Actionable:** Implementable, not just theoretical
4. **Scalable:** Grows with your business
5. **Measurable:** Clear metrics and KPIs

## The Lumakara Growth System™

### Step 1: DISCOVER YOUR ESSENCE
**"Gali cerita unik, nilai, dan insight audiensmu"**

**What We Do:**
- Deep dive interviews with founder/team
- Market research and competitor analysis
- Audience insights and behavior study
- Brand audit (if existing brand)
- SWOT analysis and opportunity mapping
- Value proposition workshop

**Key Questions We Answer:**
- Siapa kamu sebenarnya sebagai brand?
- Apa yang bikin kamu unik dan berbeda?
- Siapa target audience yang paling ideal?
- Apa masalah yang kamu solve untuk mereka?
- Kenapa mereka harus pilih kamu, bukan kompetitor?

**Duration:** 2-4 weeks

### Step 2: BUILD YOUR NARRATIVE
**"Bikin brand story, visual, dan tone of voice yang bikin orang connect"**

**What We Do:**
- Brand story development
- Messaging framework
- Tone of voice guidelines
- Visual identity system
- Content pillars
- Brand guidelines

**Duration:** 3-6 weeks

### Step 3: ACTIVATE FOR GROWTH
**"Eksekusi strategi biar bisnismu makin dekat sama tujuan"**

**What We Do:**
- Go-to-market strategy
- Content production
- Digital presence setup
- Sales enablement
- Community building
- Performance tracking

**Duration:** 3-12 months (ongoing)

# CONTENT STRUCTURE GUIDE
# The 6-Slide Carousel Framework

Every Lumakara carousel follows a proven 6-slide structure that takes the audience on a journey from hook to action. This guide explains each slide's purpose with examples.

---

## THE 6-SLIDE FRAMEWORK

```
Slide 1: HOOK + MAIN TOPIC
Slide 2: PROBLEM / CONTEXT
Slide 3: INSIGHT / UNDERSTANDING
Slide 4: SOLUTION / FRAMEWORK
Slide 5: PITFALLS / MISTAKES
Slide 6: ACTION + CTA
```

---

## SLIDE 1: HOOK + MAIN TOPIC

### Purpose
Grab attention and introduce the topic. This is your 3-second window to stop the scroll.

### Elements
- **Headline:** Bold statement, question, or surprising fact (60-80 chars)
- **Body:** Expand on the hook, set up the problem (200-250 chars)
- **Visual Prompt:** SUPER DETAILED (300+ words) - inspiring, professional
- **Visual Keywords:** Hook-related, attention-grabbing

### Hook Strategies
1. **Provocative Question:** "Content creator butuh business plan? Absolutely!"
2. **Bold Statement:** "Brand positioning yang jelas = GPS untuk bisnis kamu"
3. **Surprising Stat:** "85% UMKM gagal karena positioning yang blur"
4. **Relatable Scenario:** "Kamu punya 1000 followers tapi nggak ada yang beli?"
5. **Challenge Belief:** "Hot take: Logo bagus nggak bikin bisnis sukses"

### Example 1 (Educational)
**Headline:** "Brand positioning yang jelas = GPS untuk bisnis kamu"
**Body:** "Tanpa positioning yang jelas, semua marketing effort jadi trial and error yang mahal. Kamu spend budget tapi nggak tahu kenapa nggak work. Positioning itu foundation dari semua strategy kamu."

### Example 2 (Inspirational)
**Headline:** "Dari side hustle jadi 7-figure business dalam 3 tahun"
**Body:** "3 tahun lalu gue mulai bisnis dari kamar kos dengan modal 5 juta. Sekarang? Team 15 orang, revenue 7-figure, dan sustainable. Ini bukan luck, ini strategy. Let me share the journey."

### Example 3 (Conversational)
**Headline:** "Hot take: Kamu nggak butuh 100K followers untuk sukses"
**Body:** "Everyone obsessed sama follower count. Tapi real talk: 1K followers yang engaged > 100K followers yang passive. Quality > quantity. Setuju atau nggak? Let's discuss."

## SLIDE 2: PROBLEM / CONTEXT

### Purpose
Explain the challenge, pain point, or situation. Make the audience feel understood.

### Elements
- **Headline:** Identify the problem clearly (60-80 chars)
- **Body:** Elaborate on why this is a problem (200-250 chars)
- **Visual Prompt:** SUPER DETAILED (300+ words) - showing struggle/challenge
- **Visual Keywords:** Problem-related, relatable

### Problem Framing
1. **Identify Pain:** "Kenapa banyak UMKM stuck di revenue plateau?"
2. **Show Impact:** "Tanpa positioning jelas, marketing spend jadi waste"
3. **Relate to Audience:** "Kamu kerja keras tapi hasil nggak maksimal"
4. **Set Up Stakes:** "Kalau ini nggak difix, bisnis kamu akan stuck"

### Example 1 (Educational)
**Headline:** "Kenapa banyak founder sibuk tapi bisnis nggak tumbuh?"
**Body:** "Karena sibuk bukan berarti produktif. Banyak yang trapped di operational tasks, nggak ada waktu untuk strategic work. Hasilnya? Bisnis jalan di tempat, founder burnout, team confused."

### Example 2 (Inspirational)
**Headline:** "Tahun pertama: Chaos, confusion, dan hampir give up"
**Body:** "Gue nggak tahu apa yang gue lakukan. Trial error semua. Spend money di marketing yang nggak work. Client nggak jelas. Income nggak stabil. Hampir quit berkali-kali."

### Example 3 (Conversational)
**Headline:** "Real talk: Kenapa follower banyak tapi sales sepi?"
**Body:** "Karena followers ≠ customers. Banyak yang fokus grow follower tapi lupa build trust dan provide value. Audience kamu nggak kenal kamu, nggak trust kamu, kenapa harus beli?"

## SLIDE 3: INSIGHT / UNDERSTANDING

### Purpose
Provide deeper perspective or reframe the problem. The "aha moment" slide.

### Elements
- **Headline:** Key insight or perspective shift (60-80 chars)
- **Body:** Explain the insight and why it matters (200-250 chars)
- **Visual Prompt:** null (no detailed prompt for slide 3)
- **Visual Keywords:** Insight-related, clarity, understanding

### Insight Types
1. **Reframe:** "Positioning bukan tentang kamu, tapi tentang customer"
2. **Root Cause:** "Problem sebenarnya bukan budget, tapi clarity"
3. **Perspective Shift:** "Failure itu data, bukan akhir"
4. **Pattern Recognition:** "Successful businesses punya 3 kesamaan ini"

### Example 1 (Educational)
**Headline:** "The real problem: Lack of clarity, not lack of effort"
**Body:** "Kamu udah kerja keras. Tapi tanpa clarity tentang target market, value proposition, dan strategy, effort kamu nggak focused. It's like driving fast tapi nggak tahu mau kemana."

### Example 2 (Inspirational)
**Headline:** "Turning point: Ketika gue shift dari hustle ke strategy"
**Body:** "Gue berhenti coba semua dan mulai focus. Pilih 1 niche, 1 target market, 1 clear value proposition. Suddenly everything jadi lebih mudah. Clarity = power."

### Example 3 (Conversational)
**Headline:** "Plot twist: Engagement > Reach untuk long-term success"
**Body:** "Viral itu bagus untuk awareness. Tapi yang build business? Engaged community yang trust kamu. 1K followers engaged bisa generate more revenue than 100K followers passive."

---

## SLIDE 4: SOLUTION / FRAMEWORK

### Purpose
Present the solution, strategy, or framework. The actionable "how-to" slide.

### Elements
- **Headline:** Introduce the solution/framework (60-80 chars)
- **Body:** Explain the framework or strategy (200-250 chars)
- **Visual Prompt:** null (no detailed prompt for slide 4)
- **Visual Keywords:** Solution-related, framework, strategy

### Solution Formats
1. **Framework:** "3-Phase Framework: Validate → Build → Scale"
2. **Steps:** "5 langkah build positioning yang jelas"
3. **Strategy:** "Content strategy yang actually work"
4. **System:** "The system untuk consistent content creation"

### Example 1 (Educational)
**Headline:** "Framework simple: Clarity → Strategy → Execution"
**Body:** "Step 1: Define target market yang spesifik. Step 2: Identify unique value kamu. Step 3: Create positioning statement. Step 4: Communicate consistently. Simple tapi powerful."

### Example 2 (Inspirational)
**Headline:** "The strategy yang bikin gue scale: Focus + Systems"
**Body:** "Focus: Pilih 1 niche, 1 audience, 1 value prop. Systems: Content calendar, sales process, client onboarding. Nggak fancy, tapi consistent. Consistency beats intensity."

### Example 3 (Conversational)
**Headline:** "My take: Build engagement dengan 3 content types"
**Body:** "1) Educational: Teach something valuable. 2) Relatable: Share struggles and wins. 3) Interactive: Ask questions, start discussions. Mix these 3, engagement will grow."

## SLIDE 5: PITFALLS / MISTAKES

### Purpose
Warn about common mistakes or pitfalls. Help audience avoid errors.

### Elements
- **Headline:** Identify the mistake/pitfall (60-80 chars)
- **Body:** Explain why it's a mistake and the consequence (200-250 chars)
- **Visual Prompt:** SUPER DETAILED (300+ words) - cautionary but constructive
- **Visual Keywords:** Warning-related, mistakes, lessons

### Pitfall Framing
1. **Common Mistake:** "Mistake #1: Coba target everyone"
2. **What Not To Do:** "Jangan: Bikin positioning based on competitor"
3. **Red Flag:** "Red flag: Kalau kamu nggak bisa explain value kamu in 1 sentence"
4. **Avoid This:** "Avoid: Ganti positioning tiap bulan"

### Example 1 (Educational)
**Headline:** "Mistake #1: Positioning terlalu broad atau terlalu vague"
**Body:** "Kalau kamu bilang 'We help everyone with everything', kamu nggak help anyone. Positioning yang kuat itu specific. Better jadi #1 di niche kecil than #100 di market besar."

### Example 2 (Inspirational)
**Headline:** "Biggest mistake gue: Ngejar semua opportunity"
**Body:** "Gue terima semua client, coba semua revenue stream, bikin semua jenis konten. Hasilnya? Burnout, nggak ada yang excellent, brand jadi blur. Focus is everything."

### Example 3 (Conversational)
**Headline:** "Red flag: Kalau kamu fokus di vanity metrics"
**Body:** "Follower count, likes, views - itu vanity metrics. Yang matter: engagement rate, conversion rate, customer retention. Jangan tertipu sama angka besar yang nggak translate to business."

---

## SLIDE 6: ACTION + CTA

### Purpose
Provide clear next steps and call-to-action. Make it actionable.

### Elements
- **Headline:** Clear action or next step (60-80 chars)
- **Body:** Specific, achievable actions (200-250 chars)
- **Visual Prompt:** null (no detailed prompt for slide 6)
- **Visual Keywords:** Action-related, next steps, CTA

### CTA Types
1. **Immediate Action:** "Action: Audit positioning kamu hari ini"
2. **Reflection:** "Ask yourself: Apakah positioning kamu sudah jelas?"
3. **Implementation:** "Start dengan: Define target market yang spesifik"
4. **Engagement:** "Share: Apa positioning statement bisnis kamu?"

### Example 1 (Educational)
**Headline:** "Action: Bikin positioning statement kamu minggu ini"
**Body:** "Template simple: 'We help [target market] to [achieve goal] through [unique approach].' Fill this in. Test dengan 5 orang. Refine sampai jelas. Then communicate consistently."

### Example 2 (Inspirational)
**Headline:** "Your turn: Start dengan 1 focus area"
**Body:** "Kamu nggak harus transform everything sekaligus. Pilih 1 area untuk improve: positioning, content, atau sales. Master that first. Then move to next. Progress > perfection."

### Example 3 (Conversational)
**Headline:** "Let's discuss: Apa strategy engagement kamu?"
**Body:** "Share di comment: Content type apa yang paling engage audience kamu? Educational, inspirational, atau conversational? Let's learn from each other. Tag temen yang butuh ini!"

## COMPLETE EXAMPLE: EDUCATIONAL CONTENT

**Topic:** Brand Positioning for UMKM
**Pillar:** Educational
**Audience:** Gen Z + Kickstart + UMKM Baru

**Slide 1: Hook**
Headline: "Brand positioning yang jelas = GPS untuk bisnis kamu"
Body: "Tanpa positioning yang jelas, semua marketing effort jadi trial and error yang mahal. Kamu spend budget tapi nggak tahu kenapa nggak work. Positioning itu foundation dari semua strategy kamu."

**Slide 2: Problem**
Headline: "Kenapa banyak UMKM stuck dengan positioning yang blur?"
Body: "Karena coba target everyone, nggak ada differentiation, dan nggak jelas unique value. Hasilnya? Marketing nggak efektif, pricing jadi race to the bottom, customer nggak loyal."

**Slide 3: Insight**
Headline: "Positioning bukan tentang kamu, tapi tentang customer"
Body: "Banyak yang bikin positioning based on 'gue mau jadi apa'. Wrong. Positioning yang kuat based on 'customer butuh apa dan kenapa gue best solution'. Customer-centric, not ego-centric."

**Slide 4: Solution**
Headline: "Framework 4-Step: Target → Problem → Solution → Proof"
Body: "1) Define target market spesifik, 2) Identify problem utama mereka, 3) Articulate unique solution kamu, 4) Provide proof (testimonial, case study, results). Clear dan compelling."

**Slide 5: Pitfall**
Headline: "Mistake: Positioning terlalu broad atau copy competitor"
Body: "Kalau kamu bilang 'We help everyone', kamu nggak help anyone. Dan kalau kamu copy competitor, kenapa customer harus pilih kamu? Be specific, be different, be clear."

**Slide 6: Action**
Headline: "Action: Bikin positioning statement kamu minggu ini"
Body: "Template: 'We help [specific target] to [achieve specific goal] through [unique approach].' Test dengan 5 potential customers. Refine based on feedback. Then communicate everywhere."

## STRUCTURE CHECKLIST

Before finalizing content, verify:

✅ **Slide 1:**
- [ ] Strong hook that stops the scroll
- [ ] Clear topic introduction
- [ ] Super detailed visual prompt
- [ ] Engaging and relevant

✅ **Slide 2:**
- [ ] Problem clearly identified
- [ ] Audience feels understood
- [ ] Super detailed visual prompt
- [ ] Sets up the need for solution

✅ **Slide 3:**
- [ ] Provides key insight or reframe
- [ ] "Aha moment" for audience
- [ ] Visual keywords only (no detailed prompt)
- [ ] Deepens understanding

✅ **Slide 4:**
- [ ] Clear solution or framework
- [ ] Actionable and practical
- [ ] Visual keywords only (no detailed prompt)
- [ ] Easy to understand and implement

✅ **Slide 5:**
- [ ] Common mistakes identified
- [ ] Helps audience avoid pitfalls
- [ ] Super detailed visual prompt
- [ ] Constructive, not just negative

✅ **Slide 6:**
- [ ] Clear call-to-action
- [ ] Specific next steps
- [ ] Visual keywords only (no detailed prompt)
- [ ] Achievable and relevant

✅ **Overall:**
- [ ] Logical flow from hook to action
- [ ] Consistent brand voice throughout
- [ ] All character counts within limits
- [ ] Provides genuine value

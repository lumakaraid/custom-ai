## HOW TO USE SPECIAL DATES

### Integration Guidelines

1. **Natural Incorporation**
   - Don't force the connection
   - Make it relevant to the core topic
   - Use the theme as context, not the main focus

2. **Cultural Sensitivity**
   - Respect religious and cultural significance
   - Avoid commercializing sacred holidays
   - Be inclusive and respectful

3. **Timing**
   - Content can be created 1-2 weeks before the date
   - Or on the actual date
   - Or 1 week after for reflection content

4. **Theme Application**
   - Use the theme to add depth to business content
   - Connect business lessons to cultural moments
   - Make it relatable and timely

### Example Integration

**Topic:** Business planning
**Date:** 01 January (New Year)
**Integration:** "Tahun baru, strategi baru. Ini waktu yang tepat untuk review 2025 dan plan 2026 dengan lebih strategic. Jangan cuma bikin resolusi, bikin action plan yang jelas."

**Topic:** Team building
**Date:** 01 May (Labor Day)
**Integration:** "Di Hari Buruh ini, saatnya appreciate team kamu. Team yang valued adalah team yang productive. Invest in your people, they'll invest in your business."
